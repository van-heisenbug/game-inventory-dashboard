About this game

You play Zagreus, the restless son of the god of the dead, trying to fight your way out of the Underworld. Every escape attempt is a new run through shifting chambers full of enemies, and every failure sends you back home a little stronger.

Pick up gifts from the gods of Olympus, change your weapon to suit your style, and get to know the family you’re running from. The more you die, the more you learn.
